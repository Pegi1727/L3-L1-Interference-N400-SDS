## Figure 5 — Fig5_Behavioral_Summary_300dpi.png (خلاصه رفتاری/ارتباط مغز–رفتار)

**متن قبل:** [177] "Change in N400 amplitude was positively associated with change in L1 calque errors (r = .48, p = .002) and negatively associated with change in oral fluency (r = −.41, p = .009). Change in RT was positively associated with change in L1 calque errors (r = .44, p = .004)."
**متن بعد:** [178] "These correlations are reported as longitudinal associations and should not be interpreted as evidence of causal or mechanistic relationships."
